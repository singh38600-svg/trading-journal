## 1. The Authentic Product Discovery Moment

**Why it works**: UGC-style "I can't believe this exists" reactions drive insane conversion. Feels real, not produced.

**Viral mechanic**: First 2 seconds = relatable confusion → instant hook → product reveal

**Image Prompt (NanoBanana 2):**
```
24-year-old woman with loose wavy auburn hair, minimal makeup, wearing an oversized cream hoodie.
Sitting cross-legged on a white bed, soft morning light from window behind her.
Holding a small product box, eyebrows slightly raised in genuine surprise.
iPhone selfie angle, slightly messy room in background, authentic and unposed.
9:16, 2K quality.
```

**Video Prompt (Seedance 2.0):**
```
@image1 — Woman looks down at the product in her hands, slowly looks up at the camera
with a growing smile, mouths "wait... what?" — soft natural light, cozy bedroom.
Handheld camera slight movement for authenticity. Golden morning glow.
8 seconds, 9:16.
```

**Hook text overlay**: "I wasn't expecting THIS"

## 2. The Before/After Glow-Up

**Why it works**: Transformation content is the #1 performing format on TikTok and Reels consistently.

**Viral mechanic**: Pattern interrupt — unexpected or dramatic transformation in under 8 seconds

**Image Prompt (NanoBanana 2):**
```
Split creative: left half shows woman with tired expression, no makeup, messy bun,
plain grey t-shirt, bathroom mirror reflection. Right half same woman — glowing skin,
soft natural makeup, silk blouse, confident relaxed expression. Warm bathroom lighting.
Photorealistic, 9:16.
```

**Video Prompt (Seedance 2.0):**
```
@image1 — Camera slowly pushes in on the woman's face as soft golden light increases in intensity,
a subtle digital shimmer effect as she transitions from left to right frame state — her eyes
brighten and she smiles slowly. Cinematic rack focus. 8 seconds, 9:16.
```

**Hook text overlay**: "POV: Week 1 vs Week 4"
